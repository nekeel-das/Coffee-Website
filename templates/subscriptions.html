FAQ

Common questions

Can I change my plan after subscribing?

Yes! You can upgrade, downgrade or change your grind preference at any time from your account settings. Changes take effect from your next billing cycle.

How do I pause or skip a delivery?

Log into your account and navigate to Subscriptions. You can skip the next shipment or pause for up to 3 months with a single click — no questions asked.

What if I don't like a particular coffee?

We want you to love every cup. If you receive a coffee that doesn't suit your taste, contact us within 14 days and we'll send a replacement — at no extra cost.

Do you ship outside India?

Currently our subscriptions are available across India only. International shipping for one-off orders is coming soon — sign up to be notified.
